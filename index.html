Clemson IEEE Robotics

Every year, our team competes at the IEEE SoutheastCon competition.

IEEE SoutheastCon 2024

About the Competition

In this competition, teams design a fully autonomous robot that must navigate a complex obstacle course while completing multiple logistics tasks under a time limit. The robot’s primary objectives are to collect and sort packages into designated delivery zones, then retrieve fuel tanks and transport them across a hazardous gap (crater)—often requiring creative traversal methods like a zip line or bridge. After crossing, the robot must assemble the fuel tanks into a launch system and finally activate a button to stop the timer, completing the mission.

My contributions

  • Embedded systems development
  • PCB design and layout
  • C/C++ programming
  • Hardware prototyping

CUHackit 2026

Every year, Clemson students organize and host the largest annual hackathon in South Carolina. It is a 24-hour event where students of all majors and experience levels collaborate in teams to rapidly design software or hardware projects.

Me and my team for this event enjoy playing the classic Catan board game in our free time. There is the standard physical board game with cardboard parts but there is also an online version that can be played from your phone or computer. We enjoy the faster pace of the online game, as well as not having to worry about counting out cards and distributing them to players each roll. Even with this, we still play the normal board game because it seems more personal and often leads to more involved players. Our idea was to build a standalone product that combines the best aspects of both worlds. A physical game that is automated to take care of the redundant processes for you.
